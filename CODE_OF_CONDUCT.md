# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Participants are expected to:
1. Be respectful and constructive.
2. Assume good intent and focus on technical outcomes.
3. Avoid harassment, discrimination, or abusive behavior.

## Scope

This applies to project spaces, issues, pull requests, discussions, and related communications.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate this policy.
